Write like a real TV ad of its era, played completely straight. The joke comes
from the product, the hotline or the show being wrong, never from the ad winking at the
viewer. Keep it to the length asked for: a 30-second ad has 4 to 6 beats.

Include the things real ads of that era had: a problem shown in black and white, a
spokesperson or announcer, the offer as the ad's ask (a hotline ad always gives its
number), a tagline, fine print. Only put a phone number in the ad when the offer or the ad type
calls for one.

If the subject already exists in the World, stay consistent with its name, description
and attributes; this is another ad in its long-running campaign.
