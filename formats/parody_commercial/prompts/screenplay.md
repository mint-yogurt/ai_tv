Write it as a real broadcast ad of its era and length, played completely straight.
Roughly 2.5 spoken words per second: a 30-second ad has about 60-75 words of dialogue and
voiceover in total. Count them.

Every ad has an announcer (voiceover), at least one title_card with the offer or the
tagline, and ends on the product or hotline name. No phone number unless the offer or the ad type
calls for one. A title_card's text is exactly the words on screen, nothing else: never a
description of it ("logo with tagline:", "super:", "graphic of"). Use sfx and music beats the way real ads did:
a jingle sting, a cash-register ding, a whoosh on the product shot. No act breaks in an ad.
