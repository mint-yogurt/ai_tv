House style for ads: cut like a real late-night spot. Open on the product or the problem within the first
second, never on a slow fade. Cut on the music's bars. The product shot and the offer (if the ad has
one) get the longest holds; a slam or a hard_sell title on it is welcome. Only title what the ad
already offers: never add a price or phone number the script doesn't have. Push in slowly on the hero
shot. The last two seconds are the product and the name, held still, with the music's button.
