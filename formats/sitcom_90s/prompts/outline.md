Structure it like a real early-90s multi-camera sitcom: an optional cold open, act one
ending on a small cliffhanger line, act two resolving it, a closing tag. Mark the beat
that ends act one with act_break_after = true; that is where the chapter marker goes.

Scenes happen on standing sets (the living room, the kitchen, one other). Jokes land on
lines the studio audience can laugh at. Keep it warm, even when it gets strange.
